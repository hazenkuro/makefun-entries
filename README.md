Lumen Drift is a neon 2D canvas arcade game: you pilot a small light-skiff through a solar storm field, steering with arrows/WASD or the mouse and dashing with Space or a click. Collect glowing star shards to build a combo multiplier (up to x5) and reach 1,200 points to open the Aurora Gate and win, while dodging drifting void mines and fast storm shards — three hull hearts stand between you and the storm, with an occasional shield cell granting one free hit. Difficulty ramps through five levels as your score climbs, over a parallax starfield with particle trails, explosions, and WebAudio bleeps generated in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Or serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`.
